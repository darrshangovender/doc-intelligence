MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "MSA") is made effective on
15 March 2025 between MediBridge Health Solutions ("MediBridge") and
LeasEase Property Tech (Pty) Ltd ("LeasEase").

TERM: The initial term of this MSA is twelve (12) months from the
Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive 12-month
periods unless either party provides 60 days' prior written notice of
non-renewal.

GOVERNING LAW: This MSA shall be governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of the Western Cape, South Africa.

This MSA constitutes a Master Services Agreement under which Statements
of Work may be executed by the parties from time to time.
